Title: Bug Patch
Summary: Mod compatibility fixes

## Compatibility
- Fixed "GL_INVALID_OPERATION in glDrawBuffers" log spam with Iris shaders on Minecraft 1.21.5 and above
- Fixed rendering issues on AMD GPUs when joining a world with Iris shaders on Minecraft 1.21.5 and above
- Fixed crash with mods that JarJar Kotlin libraries when KotlinForForge is not installed on Minecraft 1.17 and above
